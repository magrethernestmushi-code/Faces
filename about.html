ABOUT

YOU HAVE
MANY FACES.

We started FACES because nobody wears one version of themselves. Confident in public, uncertain in private. Playful with friends, serious at work. None of that makes you fake — it makes you human. FACES is the brand that holds space for all of it, stitched into fabric you can actually wear.

PHILOSOPHY

Every person has different sides, shaped by environment, relationships, ambitions, and culture. FACES doesn't ask you to pick one — it gives every side somewhere to show up.

THE MARKS

The graffiti wordmark, the crowned emblem, and the GRANDPOOBAH globe aren't three separate brands — they're three dialects of the same language: self-expression, worn loud.

WHERE WE'RE FROM

Built out of Dar es Salaam, for a community that moves between streetwear, music, sneakers, and art without ever picking a lane.
